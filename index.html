Question of the Week

Ask anything about design, anonymously. Each week the student panel picks one and the VCD team answers it here.

How do I make my poster look less "default"?

Asked anonymously by a Year 9 student · Week 3, Term 4

Great question. Most "default" looks come from using every option at once. Try limiting yourself:

  1. One typeface, two weights. Let size and weight do the work, not five fonts.
  2. Make one thing huge. Decide what someone should see first and scale it up until it feels too big.
  3. Three colours, maximum. Pull them from a photo or a palette on Coolors.
  4. Use the edges. Align everything to a margin. Centring everything is the fastest way to look default.

Answered by the VCD team

Earlier questions

Can I use Google Fonts in my folio?

Yes. They're open-licence, so they're free to use. Write down where each font came from in your folio, the same way you'd note any image source.

Photopea keeps freezing on my laptop. Help?

Work at a smaller size while you're drafting, merge layers you've finished with, and close other tabs. Save the full-size version for your final export, on a school laptop if you need to.

Where do designers get ideas from?

Mostly from looking at a lot of things. Start in the Reading Room, save what you like, and write one sentence about why each one works.

How it's run

Owned by the VCD department, curated with students. Here's how a tool gets onto the shelf, and who keeps it working.

Every tool must pass all five

  • Free for students, or covered by a school licence.
  • Runs in a browser on our lowest-spec school laptop. No installs.
  • Safe to sign up for. No account, or school sign-in, or terms checked for age and data.
  • Legal. No piracy, ripping or cracked software, ever.
  • Checked every term. Links tested and the date shown on each tool.
